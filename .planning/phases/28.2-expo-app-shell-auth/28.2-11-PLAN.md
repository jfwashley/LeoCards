<planner_decisions>
- **This plan sets `headerShown: false` on the tab navigator; plan 28.2-12 adds the header.** The
  header's whole purpose is the account glyph that opens the sign-out sheet (D-27), and that depends on
  the auth client and the sheet component. Splitting them keeps each plan's diff coherent and lets this
  one land as pure navigation structure.
- **Tab glyphs are hand-drawn `react-native-svg` shapes, not an icon font.** The web codebase draws
  every glyph by hand (`AccountGlyph`, `HBack`) and explicitly avoids emoji and icon libraries;
  `react-native-svg` is already a dependency for Leo, so this adds no package. 28.2-UI-SPEC.md §Design
  Decisions Log signs this off.
- **All three teasers share one component with per-tab copy.** D-28 asks for the same treatment on each
  tab; three near-identical screens would drift the moment one is edited. It also means 28.3 and 28.4
  each delete one usage rather than untangling three bespoke layouts.
- **No CTA on any teaser.** There is nothing to do yet. 28.2-UI-SPEC.md §10.7 is explicit that these are
  placeholders, not actionable empty states — adding a button would promise something the phase cannot
  deliver.
- **The route file names are `deck`, `study` and `habitat`, in that order.** The order is the tab order
  from D-26 and it should not be rearranged later; expo-router orders tabs by the `Tabs.Screen`
  declarations in the layout.
</planner_decisions>

<interfaces>
**Tab bar (28.2-UI-SPEC.md §10.6):** background `#FFFFFF`, 1px top border in `#EDDFC9`. Each tab shows
a 24×24 glyph above a 12/600 label. Inactive is ink `#4A331C` for both glyph and label; active is amber
`#F28A1F` for both, with the glyph filled rather than outlined. Amber appears nowhere else in the
chrome, which is what makes the current section unambiguous. testIDs `tab-bar-deck`, `tab-bar-study`,
`tab-bar-habitat`.

**Glyph motifs (28.2-UI-SPEC.md §10.6):**
- Deck — two overlapping rounded rectangles, reading as a flashcard stack
- Study — an open book: two page shapes meeting at a spine
- Habitat — a simple rounded hill or mound, echoing the habitat's own mound motif

Each is flat geometry on a 24-unit viewBox. The focused state is a fill; the unfocused state is a
stroke in ink. Keep them recognisable at 24pt — two or three shapes each, no fine detail.

**Teaser template (28.2-UI-SPEC.md §10.7):** cream `#FFF6E9` background filling the content area
between header and tab bar; a centred column with `LionFace size={72}`, a 16 gap, the tab name at the
theme's heading role (Baloo 2 700 / 22) in ink, an 8 gap, and the line at the body role (Figtree 400 /
14 / line-height 21) in muted `#9C8467`, centred and capped at 260 wide.

Copy, exactly (28.2-UI-SPEC.md §Copywriting Contract):

| Route | Heading | Line | testID |
|---|---|---|---|
| deck | `Deck` | `Your words are gathering here soon.` | `teaser-deck` |
| study | `Study` | `Your next session is warming up here soon.` | `teaser-study` |
| habitat | `Habitat` | `Your habitat is growing here soon.` | `teaser-habitat` |

The Habitat line is Josh's own example from CONTEXT §Specifics; the other two mirror its grammatical
shape ("Your {noun} is/are {verb-ing} here soon"). 28.2-UI-SPEC.md §10.7 carries one open note: Josh's
quoted example has no trailing full stop, and the UI-SPEC added one to all three for grammatical
consistency. Ship them with the full stop as written above; if Josh raises it at the phase's
human-verify gate, defer to his literal string.

**Composition source:** the teaser's shape — a scenic background with a centred Leo and one line of
warm copy — is the same composition `DaybreakAuthScene` already uses (28.2-PATTERNS.md Group D). Reuse
the proportions and the tone; do not rebuild the auth scene's gradient, hills or grass here, which
would make a placeholder heavier than the real screens that replace it.
</interfaces>
