<objective>
Downscale photos client-side (~1568 px long edge, JPEG q0.8) before upload so a 5 MB phone photo becomes a few hundred KB, and close the silent 3.3-5 MB dead zone (server 7 MB cap vs Vercel's ~4.5 MB body limit) by lowering the server cap to 4 MB while loosening the client acceptance cap to ~20 MB (D-06/D-07). Zero new dependencies — native `createImageBitmap` + canvas + `toBlob`.

Purpose: Uploads shrink by an order of magnitude; the dead band where a body passes the 7 MB app cap but is rejected by Vercel's ~4.5 MB limit can no longer exist.
Output: A new isolated resize helper + jsdom test, updated caps/copy across constants + validation + the 413 friendly message, resize wired into the upload flow, and the e2e cap assertion retargeted.
</objective>
