    - returns 1 when effectiveCardCount is 0 (D-08: new users start at level 1)
    - returns 1 when effectiveCardCount is 4 (below first threshold of 5)
    - returns 1 when effectiveCardCount is 5 (at first threshold — level 1 means you NEED 5 to reach level 2... wait, thresholds define when you REACH that level)
    - Threshold interpretation: LEVEL_THRESHOLDS = [5, 15, 30, 50, 80, 120, 170, 230, 300, 400] where reaching threshold[i] means level i+1. So 0-4 cards = level 1, 5-14 = level 2, 15-29 = level 3, etc.
    - Wait — research says level starts at 1 and the loop does level = i + 1. So: 0 cards = level 1 (no threshold met). 5 cards >= threshold[0]=5 so level = 1 (i=0, level=0+1=1). That means the loop starts level at 1 and sets level = i+1 when effectiveCardCount >= threshold[i]. With thresholds [5,15,...], 5 cards: i=0 met (level=1), i=1 not met. So level=1. But that means 0 cards and 5 cards are BOTH level 1.
    - Correct interpretation per D-07/D-08: Level 1 is the starter. Thresholds are for levels 2-10 (need 5 for level 2, 15 for level 3, etc.). So LEVEL_THRESHOLDS[0]=5 means "reach level 2 at 5 cards".
    - Test: 0 cards -> level 1, 4 cards -> level 1, 5 cards -> level 2, 14 cards -> level 2, 15 cards -> level 3, 399 cards -> level 9, 400 cards -> level 10, 1000 cards -> level 10 (D-10: max)

    - Decayed user (10 days inactive, 100 learned cards): quality = 1.0 - (10-2)*0.05 = 0.60. effective = floor(0.60 * 100) = 60. Level: 60 >= 50 (level 5), 60 < 80 (level 6). So level 5, mood neutral (quality 0.60 >= 0.40, < 0.75)
